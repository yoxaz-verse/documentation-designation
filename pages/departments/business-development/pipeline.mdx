## Sales feedback loop

<SalesFeedback />

After the meeting, Sales must update the shared record. Otherwise BD may continue contacting the Associate without knowing the result. Maintain one Associate record containing company and contact information, category, products, geography, BD owner, conversation history, status, follow-up date, registration status, meeting details, Sales owner, notes and final outcome.
