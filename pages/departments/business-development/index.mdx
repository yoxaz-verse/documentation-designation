## Department purpose

The BD team is the first human interaction between OBAOL Supreme and a potential Associate. Its objective is to identify the right people, begin a relevant conversation, explain OBAOL briefly, guide them to the website, qualify interest and schedule a contextual meeting for Sales.

<BDProcess />

## What counts as an Associate?

<AssociateTypes />

The conversation must change according to the Associate. A supplier should be asked about products supplied; a buyer about commodities sourced; an exporter about markets served; and a logistics partner about service and regional coverage.

## Department boundary

<Boundary />

BD must not explain every feature, negotiate commercial terms or try to close the Associate. Sales owns deep discovery, objections, commercial discussion and conversion.

## Team structure

- **BD Intern / BDA:** prospecting, LinkedIn outreach, initial conversations, basic qualification, website communication, follow-ups, scheduling and CRM updates.
- **BD Team Lead:** target allocation, conversation quality, distribution, training, daily monitoring, pipeline review and Sales coordination.
- **Sales Team:** meetings, requirement discovery, detailed qualification, commercial conversations, conversion and onboarding coordination.

> **Research finds opportunities. BD creates relationships. Sales converts opportunities. Associate Relations maintains the relationship.**
