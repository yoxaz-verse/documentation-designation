## Acquisition channels

| Channel | Contribution | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BD – LinkedIn | Identifies relevant businesses and creates initial interest | Detailed relationship workflow remains in the BD department |
| Lead Research | Researches expos, events and participant lists | Separate from Strategic Research opportunities and institutional work |
| Social / eMarketing | Generates educational inbound interest and enquiries | Does not qualify or promise commercial outcomes |
| Email Outbound | Sends approved targeted outreach and routes replies | Must use authorized accounts and templates |

All channels feed one central lead pool. A lead is not ready for allocation until its source, relevance, category, duplicate check and proposed next action are recorded.
