## Department model

- Four research functions: funding, ecosystem, institutions and events.
- Three execution functions: applications, college outreach and external outreach.
- One coordinator for review, assignment, quality and accountability.
- One central workspace for linked records, tasks, deadlines and results.

Start with the four research workstreams, one coordinator and clear execution ownership. Selected interns may hold multiple functions initially. Expand staffing only after consistent opportunity flow reveals actual bottlenecks.

<Principle />

Success is not measured by how many websites the team visits. It is measured by how many verified opportunities become meaningful applications, institutional relationships, startup participation and business outcomes.
