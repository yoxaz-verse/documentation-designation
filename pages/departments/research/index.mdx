## Mission and scope

Identify, verify, evaluate and convert external opportunities that can accelerate OBAOL Supreme's funding, market access, credibility, institutional relationships and business growth.

## Operating architecture

<OrgArchitecture />

This is a functional architecture. At full staffing it represents one coordinator, four research interns and three execution interns. With fewer people, responsibilities may be combined **without combining the tracking categories**.

## Accountability model

- **Founder / Management:** final strategic decisions and approvals.
- **RSO Coordinator / Lead:** planning, work assignment, quality control, handoffs and reporting.
- **Research Division:** discover, verify, document and recommend opportunities.
- **Execution Division:** apply, contact, coordinate participation and follow up.
- **Central workspace:** one verified record, one accountable owner, one next action and one recorded result.
