# Execution division

Execution begins only after a qualified opportunity is reviewed and assigned. Every submission, contact and commitment must be recorded.

<ExecutionTabs />

## Communication and tools

| Tool / workspace | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Zoho Cliq | Internal discussions, assignments, urgent questions and coordination |
| Opportunity database | Official research records and pipeline states |
| Task management system | Owners, due dates, checklists and progress |
| Shared repository | Research briefs, pitch decks and approved company documents |
| Authorized company email | Formal applications and external communication |
| Department dashboard | Weekly review and management reporting |

An opportunity is not assigned merely because it was mentioned in Zoho Cliq. It must have an execution owner and a task in the tracking system.

### Suggested Zoho Cliq channels

`rso-general` · `rso-research` · `rso-applications` · `rso-college-outreach` · `rso-events-awards` · `rso-daily-updates`
